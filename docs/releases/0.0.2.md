# Daydreaming 0.0.2

Your wallpaper can follow macOS Light and Dark Mode.

- Switches your running wallpaper when macOS changes appearance. Matching saved wallpapers appear immediately; otherwise Daydreaming makes one within your daily image limit. Paused wallpapers stay unchanged.
- Adds the current macOS appearance to the AI context. Try an idea such as “Keep the scene natural in Light Mode, and use softer, darker colors in Dark Mode.”
- Choose the weather location on the main screen: your current location, a fixed town or city, or the GPS location saved in your original picture.
- Refreshes stale location readings and selects the current forecast hour more accurately, including clearer handling of partly cloudy weather.
- Gives the preview a softer shadow and simplifies the idea field and its explanation.

Requires macOS 26 or later. New images use your connected image provider and your daily image limit.
