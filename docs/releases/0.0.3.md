# Daydreaming 0.0.3

A calmer way to choose where your wallpaper gets its weather.

- Keeps the selected weather location on one compact row. Choosing a fixed place opens a separate search popover instead of stacking controls in the sidebar.
- Finds towns and cities as you type, and offers your picture's saved location without a long, truncated button.
- Closing the chooser without selecting a place keeps your existing location.
- Adds an idea example that follows the time of day and weather at your chosen location, from morning light to city lights after sunset, with softer colors in Dark Mode.

Requires macOS 26 or later.
